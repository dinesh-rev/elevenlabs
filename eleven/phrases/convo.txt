# Two-voice dialogues: "Speaker: [tag] line". Rendering these properly needs
# elevenlabs text_to_dialogue, which convo.py already uses -- the single-voice
# path would read "Rose:" and "Jack:" aloud.

# 1. Close Game
Rose: [curious] Jack, this is getting pretty tight... do you think we are heading to deuce?
Jack: [thoughtful] It is starting to feel that way. [pause] Neither side looks ready to let this game go easily.

# 2. Light Prediction
Rose: [playful] I have got a feeling we might see deuce here.
Jack: [chuckle] You are calling it already?
Rose: [lightly] Not calling it... just preparing myself.
Jack: [amused] Fair enough. At this score, I would keep that possibility open.

# 3. Pressure Building
Rose: [focused] The closer this score gets, the more I am thinking twenty-all.
Jack: [thoughtful] Same here. [pause] One good rally either way could change that, though.
Rose: [warm] That is what makes this part of the game interesting.

# 4. Friendly Disagreement
Rose: [curious] Deuce?
Jack: [confident] I am saying no.
Rose: [surprised] Really?
Jack: [chuckle] Somebody closes this before twenty-all.
Rose: [playful] Alright... I am remembering that prediction.

# 5. Audience Involved
Rose: [upbeat] Alright, Jack... and everyone watching, quick prediction. Do we get deuce in this game?
Jack: [thoughtful] I would say there is a very good chance.
Rose: [playful] I am leaning the same way. [pause] Let us see who gets this one right.

# 6. One Point Away
Rose: [focused] We are getting dangerously close to deuce territory now.
Jack: [amused] Dangerously close?
Rose: [chuckle] You know what I mean. One or two rallies and suddenly nobody has an easy finish.
Jack: [confident] Then the pressure really starts.

# 7. Human / Casual
Rose: [lightly] Jack, be honest... are you secretly hoping this goes to deuce?
Jack: [chuckle] As a commentator? Absolutely.
Rose: [laughs] I knew it.
Jack: [playful] Close badminton makes our job much more interesting.

# 8. Momentum Question
Rose: [thoughtful] If this does go to deuce, who do you think handles the pressure better?
Jack: [focused] Hard to say right now. Both have had good moments.
Rose: [curious] So you are avoiding the question?
Jack: [chuckle] Professionally avoiding it, yes.

# 9. Late Game Tension
Rose: [softly] You can feel the tension starting to build now.
Jack: [focused] Definitely. [pause] And if this reaches twenty-all, every rally becomes huge.
Rose: [curious] Think we get there?
Jack: [lightly] At the moment... I would not bet against it.

# 10. Playful Finish
Rose: [curious] Jack, deuce or no deuce?
Jack: [confident] Deuce.
Rose: [playful] Straight answer for once. I like it.
Jack: [chuckle] Ask me again after the next rally and I might change my mind.
Rose: [laughs] That sounds more like you.
